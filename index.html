WELCOME TO EducationistGuru

At Educationist Guru, we envision a world in which education is user-centered, designed around the student, and easy for students to access across all regions in India.

Our History

We opened in July of 2023. Our business is conveniently streamlining the application process for students to apply to multiple universities. We assist students on their entire admissions journey — from university and course selection to eligibility assessments, admissions counseling, and application support.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide convenient admissions support and transparent counseling to students, to help them overcome challenges to access higher education. We acknowledge that the selection of one's university and program is a crucial part of one's career.

Our Vision

We aim to give students the freedom to choose the education pathway that suits their personal academic needs and career goals the best. We do this by administering admissions support for online, regular, and distance learning educational programs.
