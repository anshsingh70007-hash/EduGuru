MEET OUR FOUNDER & CEO

The vision and commitment driving EducationistGuru forward.

Founder & CEO

Jatinder Kaur

Chief Executive Officer, EducationistGuru

At EducationistGuru, we envision a world in which higher education is user-centered, designed around the student, and easy to access across all regions in India. Under the guidance of Jatinder Kaur, EducationistGuru opened in July 2023 with a clear commitment: to streamline the application process and provide transparent, supportive counseling for students seeking Online, Regular, and Distance Learning education.

"We acknowledge that selecting the right university and academic program is one of the most critical decisions of a student's life. Our mission is to understand each student's personal needs and guide them with complete transparency."

Student-Centered Counseling

Tailored admission roadmaps based on each student's career objectives, background, and personal aspirations.

University Partnerships

Direct tie-ups with leading institutions across India for verified, accredited UG, PG, Diploma, and Ph.D. degrees.

End-to-End Support

Comprehensive assistance from form filling and document verification to admission confirmation and ongoing help.
