WELCOME TO EducationistGuru

At EducationistGuru, we envision a world in which higher education is user-centered, designed around the student, and easy to access across all regions in India. Since opening in July 2023, our mission is to streamline the admissions journey and give students clarity to achieve their career goals.

We aim to give students the freedom to choose the education pathway that suits their personal academic needs and career goals best by administering admissions support for online, regular, and distance learning educational programs.

Our History

EducationistGuru opened in July of 2023 with a clear commitment: to conveniently streamline the application process for students applying to multiple universities across India. We provide transparent counseling and end-to-end admission guidance across 15 faculties and 80+ programs.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide convenient admissions support and transparent counseling to students, to help them overcome challenges to access higher education. We acknowledge that the selection of one's university and program is a crucial part of one's career, and our team is committed to thoroughly understanding each student's unique aspirations.

Our Vision

At EducationistGuru, we envision a world in which higher education is student-centered, transparent, and effortlessly accessible across all regions in India. We strive to give every learner the freedom to choose the academic pathway—Online, Regular, or Distance—that matches their lifelong ambitions.
